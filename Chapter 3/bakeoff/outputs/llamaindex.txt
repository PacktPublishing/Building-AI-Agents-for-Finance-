- What it does not prove:

Risks & caveats
- Cyclicality: bank earnings (JPM) are more cyclical and interest-rate sensitive; tech/consumer-device earnings (AAPL) are affected by product cycles and services-growth sustainability.
- EPS basis: ensure you compare the same EPS basis (trailing vs forward). The values above are the retrieved latest price and EPS; for investment decisions use forward estimates and multi-year growth projections.
- One-off items, buybacks, or credit events can distort short-term EPS and P/E.
